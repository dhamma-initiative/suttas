THE JACKAL (2)
SIGĀLA SUTTA  SAṀYUTTA NIKĀYA 20:12

Dwelling at Sāvatthī. 'Monks, did you hear that jackal howling in the last watch of the night?'

'Yes, lord.'

'That jackal is afflicted with a disease called mange. There might be some gratitude & thankfulness in that jackal, but there might not be any gratitude & thankfulness in a certain person here claiming to be a follower of the Sakyan.

'Therefore, monks, you should train yourselves: 'We will be grateful & thankful. The least little favor done for us won't be forgotten.' That's how you should train yourselves.'
