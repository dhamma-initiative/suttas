ĀNANDA
ĀNANDA SUTTA  SAṀYUTTA NIKĀYA 9:5

On one occasion Venerable Ānanda was staying among the Kosalans in a forest grove. Now at that time, he was spending too much time devoted to instructing laypeople.

Then the devatā inhabiting the forest grove, feeling sympathy for Venerable Ānanda, desiring his benefit, desiring to bring him to his senses, approached him and recited a verse:

'Going to the bower
at the root of a tree,
placing
  unbinding
in your heart,
do jhāna, Gotama,
& don't be heedless.
 What use is this
 chitter-chatter
 to you?'

Then Venerable Ānanda, chastened by the devatā, came to his senses.
